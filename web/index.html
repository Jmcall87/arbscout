How the estimate is built

  1. Normalize. Convert lot size and currency, subtract the venue's fee.
  2. Centre. Use a median across venues — outliers don't drag it around.
  3. Spread. Median absolute deviation gives a robust volatility figure.
  4. Trend. A short least-squares fit projects the next quote, clamped to the observed range.
  5. Confidence. Grows with sample depth, shrinks with volatility and staleness.
